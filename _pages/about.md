I'm **Chema**, a PhD researcher at Nanyang Technological University (NTU) in Singapore since January 2026, supervised by [Prof. Arvind Easwaran](https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/arvinde/) and co-supervised by [Dr. Rémy Rigo-Mariani](https://g2elab.grenoble-inp.fr/fr/le-laboratoire/rrm) (G2Elab, CNRS).

My research asks how **electric-vehicle fleets can support buildings** through vehicle-to-building (V2B) services: parked EVs discharge into a building at its peak moments and cut its electricity bill. The hard part isn't the hardware. It's that the building, the fleet operator and the drivers are **independent parties with their own goals**. My thesis, *Optimization of EV Fleets for Vehicle-to-Building Services under Operational Uncertainty*, designs **contracts and coordination mechanisms** that make V2B worthwhile for all three, with guarantees that the commitment is actually kept.

So far I have built a simulation testbed for joint routing, ride-hailing and V2B, studied the trade-off between serving every ride and shaving the building's peak, and derived a building–fleet contract using distributed optimization (ADMM). Next, I'm extending this to gig drivers' participation, real-time commitment-keeping, and multi-agent guarantees. A personal motivation drives it: I want platform drivers to be treated fairly when they take part in the energy system.

Before NTU, I studied **Aerospace Engineering at Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB)**, Indonesia, and worked as a consulting assistant on airline fleet analysis.

Outside research, I'm the Director of Recreation of the 13th IGP Graduate Students' Council at NTU, and I mentor NTU's DeepSpeed autonomous racing team. In 2026 I also joined the **Singapore Climate & Energy Ventures (SCEV) Programme**, run by NTU, NUS and A\*STAR, working with **Suncube**, an NUS-originated crop-first agrivoltaic design system, on its commercial path.
