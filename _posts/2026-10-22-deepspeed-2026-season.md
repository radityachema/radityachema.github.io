This year I joined NTU DeepSpeed as a student mentor for the organization side; i.e., planning, logistics, and keeping everything running at the school. All the engineering work is done by the students themselves. One of the best aspects of my first PhD year so far has been to see them working up close.

They ran their 2026 season in three races:

**ICRA 2026 (Vienna)**

- They were 12th overall in the physical race, and had qualified sixth of seventy-eight teams in sim-racing.

**IFAC 2026 (Busan)**

- They placed fifteenth of fifty-five international teams, and made it into the head-to-head finals.

**The IROS 2026 Sim-Racing League**

- They took second place in this event, qualifying in first position, losing to the leader by 0.44 seconds with no crashes or errors.
